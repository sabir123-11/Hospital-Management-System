Introduction

A hospital built around the patient, not the paperwork.

Founded in 2004 as a 40-bed community clinic, City Care Hospital has grown into a 300-bed multi-specialty referral centre serving Cuttack and the surrounding region. Every expansion — a new wing, a new department, a new piece of equipment — has been guided by one question: does this help a patient get better care, faster?

Today our campus houses 18 specialty departments, a Level-3 ICU, a 24x7 emergency trauma centre, and a diagnostics lab accredited for same-day reporting.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted healthcare provider, recognised for clinical excellence and genuine compassion.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, evidence-based care through skilled specialists, modern technology and transparent communication.

Our Core Values

Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, Teamwork and Accountability guide every decision we make.
